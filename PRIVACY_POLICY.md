# Privacy Policy — Simple Data Face

_Last updated: September 26, 2026_

This privacy policy describes how the **Simple Data Face** watch face for Wear OS
(package `com.tunnellight.simpledataface`, "the app") handles your information.

## Summary

Simple Data Face does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

## What the app is

Simple Data Face is a watch face built with Google's Watch Face Format. It is a
resource-only package: it contains no executable code of its own. Everything it
shows is rendered on your watch by the Wear OS system.

## Data displayed on the watch face

The watch face can display the following information. It is read and drawn
entirely on your watch by Wear OS, and never leaves your device because of this app:

- **Time and date** from the watch's system clock.
- **Step count and daily step goal**, used for the optional activity ring. These
  values come from Wear OS. On some devices, Wear OS may ask you to grant a
  permission (such as physical activity access) before it shows this data.
- **Complications** you choose to add (for example, heart rate, battery, weather,
  or calendar). Complications are supplied by other apps or by the system. The watch
  face only displays what those providers send. Their handling of data is governed
  by their own privacy policies.

## Data collection and sharing

- The app does not collect any personal or device information.
- The app has no network access and does not send data to the developer or to any
  third party.
- The app contains no analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs.
- The app does not use cookies or tracking technologies.

## Settings

Customizations you make in the watch face editor (colors, activity ring, always-on
complications, and complication choices) are stored locally on your watch by Wear OS.
They are removed when you uninstall the watch face.

## Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children
under 13.
